# Project Summary

## Title

Investment Efficiency Shocks in a Real Business Cycle Framework

## Research Question

How do investment-efficiency shocks affect macroeconomic aggregates and investment volatility in a calibrated RBC/DSGE-style framework?

## Data

This project uses U.S. quarterly macroeconomic data from FRED, covering 1970–2024.

## Methodology

First, I process U.S. macroeconomic data and visualise quarterly growth rates of output, consumption, and investment.

Second, I estimate a Structural VAR with Cholesky identification to obtain empirical impulse responses.

Third, I build a calibrated RBC/DSGE-style model with TFP and investment-efficiency shocks and simulate impulse response functions using Matlab/Dynare.

## Main Findings

The simulation results suggest that investment-efficiency shocks increase the responsiveness of investment. However, the model does not fully match the volatility patterns observed in U.S. macroeconomic data.

This highlights the limitations of relying only on moment-matching for structural reliability.
